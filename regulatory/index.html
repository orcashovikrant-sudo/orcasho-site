01 · The position

The invention is the sequence, not the pill.

A doctor who writes "Dolo 650" has not manufactured paracetamol. If Dolo is unavailable, the patient takes Crocin, and the prescription still works. Orcasho works the same way. The value is in which compounds, in which combination, for which patient state, and what changes next. The physical product is a constituent that any compliant brand can supply.

Orcasho owns the decision layer. The supplement industry owns the product layer. Neither needs to change for the other to work.

Because the product layer stays with established brands, the manufacturer-side obligations that usually dominate this category (production licences, GMP, product registration, label approval) remain with those brands and never attach to Orcasho.

02 · Two delivery systems

Same protocol. Two ways to receive it.

SYSTEM A · CURATED BOX (INDIA)

We pick it. Amazon supplies it. The seal stays shut.

From the 101-compound library, the protocol selects roughly eight or nine compounds. Orcasho sources them as sealed retail packs from Amazon and ships them to the patient as one package, saving the patient the legwork.

Packs go out exactly as received. When a supplement runs out, the patient reorders it directly from Amazon.

SYSTEM B · LINKS (INDIA, US, MALAYSIA, ANYWHERE)

We prescribe. The patient buys wherever they like.

The patient receives a list of links for each compound. They buy from Amazon or any other retailer, and any equivalent brand is acceptable.

No goods cross a border through Orcasho, so the same system serves a patient in Chennai, Texas or Kuala Lumpur.

WHAT ORCASHO DOES
  • Selects compounds and combinations from the 101-compound library
  • Rotates the protocol every 20 days from patient feedback
  • Provides a doctor-issued prescription and a doctor consultation
  • Passes sealed retail packs through, or supplies purchase links
WHAT ORCASHO NEVER DOES
  • Manufacture, blend or encapsulate anything
  • Open a seal or break a label
  • Repackage, relabel or create a private-label product
  • Tie a patient to one brand
03 · The three-month programme

Rotate, retain, retarget.

A programme runs three months, which is four to five 20-day cycles. Each cycle starts with patient feedback, runs through the scoring model, and produces the next combination. Across a programme, some compounds are kept because the patient adapted well, and others are added to reach new parts of the system.

05 · We read the regulator

We went to the source. Here is what we found and how we adapted.

Licensing in this category has two sides: the product and the measurement and software around it. The product side is removed by design (section 01). For the other side, we studied the FDA's own frameworks on patient-reported outcomes and real-world AI performance, and built Orcasho to run in line with them from day one.

FDA · PATIENT-REPORTED OUTCOMES

Symptoms the patient alone can report are measurable.

What the FDA says. A patient-reported outcome is a measurement taken directly from the patient's report, and concepts known only to the patient, such as reflux or bloating, are legitimately measured this way.

Scope, stated plainly. FDA's PRO guidance addresses how patient-reported measures support claims in a medical product's labeling. Orcasho makes no such claims, so that claim framework is not triggered.

How we adapt. We use the principle anyway. Orcasho turns natural patient language ("my acidity is much better, but I still get some bloating") into structured direction, domain and intensity signals at every consultation. We use the principle as a learning architecture. We do not claim the result is an FDA-qualified endpoint.

FDA · PATIENT-FOCUSED DRUG DEVELOPMENT

Patient experience is a first-class data source.

What the FDA says. Its patient-focused programme exists to capture patient experience, perspectives, needs and priorities systematically.

How we adapt. Our 20-day cycle is that principle run as operations: 1,510 feedback entries over 36 months, each tied to the prescription that preceded it. The record is built on what patients experience, not on a fixed single endpoint.

FDA · REAL-WORLD AI PERFORMANCE

Static benchmarks are not enough. Monitor after deployment.

What the FDA says. In a September 2025 request for public comment (discussion only, not draft or final guidance), the FDA notes that many AI-enabled devices are tested on retrospective data or static benchmarks, and asks how developers monitor real-world performance, detect drift in inputs and outputs, and fold clinical outcomes and user feedback into model updates.

How we adapt. This is the question Orcasho was built around. A Real-World Outcome Layer, a trained model, is updated by every prescription and feedback pair. Human review sits in the loop, because a licensed doctor reviews and signs each prescription. Cycle-by-cycle outcomes act as the drift signal, and held-out testing, classifier validation and prospective evaluation are on the roadmap.

06 · The new FDA direction

The 2026 FDA moves toward models like ours.

In January 2026 the FDA issued updated final guidance on clinical decision support (CDS) software and general wellness, replacing the 2022 and 2019 versions. Law-firm summaries describe the shift as more practical and less burdensome. Software that supports a doctor's decision, and shows the doctor the basis for it, can sit outside the medical device definition when it meets four criteria. The FDA also stated it intends to use enforcement discretion for certain single-option recommendations. The revised text dropped language that had excluded risk scores from the exemption.

07 · Service-side obligations

Doctors, honest claims, patient data. Country by country.

Removing the product removes manufacturer duties. It does not remove the duties that come with caring for patients. We mapped them for the three countries we serve today. The pattern is the same everywhere: a licensed doctor in the loop, communication tied to the program and not to a product, and data handled to the local standard.

INDIA · LIVE

India

Clinical practice

Only a registered medical practitioner may practise telemedicine. The 2020 guidelines require patient identification, consent (implied when the patient starts the consult), consultation and consent records, and a registered prescriber. Platforms must check doctor registration. Our doctor-issued prescription and 20-day consult fit this frame.

Claims and communication

The Consumer Protection Act and CCPA guidelines on misleading ads and health influencers, the Drugs and Magic Remedies Act (cure claims for listed conditions), and the NMC's new ethical advertising guidelines for doctors, which cover testimonials and patient images. Our patient stories need documented consent and doctor-appropriate framing.

Patient data

The IT Act and SPDI Rules govern today. The DPDP Rules were notified in November 2025, with core duties (notice, consent, security, breach reporting to the Board and patients) applying from about May 2027.

Selling sealed packs (System A)

Buying and shipping retail packs makes Orcasho a seller and distributor, which in India generally means an FSSAI registration or licence as a food business operator. This is separate from the manufacturing licence we never need, and is a simple registration to hold.

UNITED STATES · LINKS ONLY

United States

Clinical practice

Telemedicine follows the patient's location. The clinician generally needs a licence in the state where the patient sits, and a foreign licence does not substitute. Pairing with a US doctor does not remove that duty for clinical work. For US patients we settle the staffing model with counsel: licensed US clinicians, or a program scoped to what needs no clinical practice.

Claims and communication

The FTC expects competent and reliable scientific evidence behind health claims. The FDA can infer a supplement's intended use from a seller's website, Amazon storefront, social posts and literature shipped with the product. So we keep disease language off product links and boxes, and tie outcomes to the doctor-supervised program.

Patient data

HIPAA covers providers and their business associates. Direct-to-consumer health services outside HIPAA fall under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule (amended 2024) and state health-data laws. We design to the stricter standard.

MALAYSIA · LINKS ONLY

Malaysia

Clinical practice

Medicine is regulated by the Medical Act 1971 and the Malaysian Medical Council. Foreign doctors need an MMC certificate to practise telemedicine, or must work through a fully registered Malaysian practitioner.

Claims and communication

The Medicines (Advertisement and Sale) Act 1956 requires Medicine Advertisements Board approval for supplement and medical-service advertising, shown as a KKLIU number, and bars public advertising for 20 scheduled conditions. Our Malaysia outreach follows this, and we avoid advertising by or through individual doctors.

Patient data

The PDPA 2010, amended in 2024 and phased in during 2025, adds a mandatory data protection officer, breach notification to the Commissioner (72 hours), explicit consent for sensitive data such as health, and cross-border transfer rules.

08 · Questions investors ask

Straight answers.

Does Orcasho need FDA approval or a manufacturing licence?

No product approval or manufacturing licence is required of Orcasho, because Orcasho does not manufacture, relabel or place its own product on the market. Every supplement is an existing retail product that carries the regulatory status its manufacturer already holds. Orcasho selects among them, the way a prescriber selects among brands.

Who is responsible for quality and labelling?

The brand manufacturer, exactly as in any ordinary retail purchase. Orcasho never alters the pack, so the label the patient sees is the label the manufacturer approved.

Do the supplements need a prescription?

No, they are available over the counter. Orcasho still has a licensed doctor issue a prescription and run consultations, so every protocol has clinical oversight and a written record.

What if a brand is unavailable or unsatisfactory?

The protocol is defined by compound, not brand. Any equivalent product can replace it, and patients may choose their own. There is no single supplier to lose.

How does the model differ by country?

It does not. India patients may receive a curated box or links. US and Malaysia patients receive links. The prescription logic, the 20-day loop and the doctor involvement are identical.

So do you need any licence at all?

No manufacturing, product-approval or product-labelling licence. The service side still has its own requirements: registered doctors, honest communication and data protection in each country, and in India a seller registration for System A. Section 07 maps each one.

Have you studied the FDA's frameworks on outcomes and AI?

Yes. We reviewed the FDA's materials on patient-reported outcomes, patient-focused development and real-world AI performance, and mapped each to a feature of the engine (section 05). We use the principles, and we do not overclaim: our outcome metric is not an FDA-qualified endpoint, and the FDA's AI material is open for comment, not final guidance. We track it and design ahead of it.

What do you actually own, then?

The selection logic (15-marker ionic state vector and cosine selection across 101 compounds), the 20-day intervention-response record on each retained patient, and the operating system that connects them. All of it sits above the product.
