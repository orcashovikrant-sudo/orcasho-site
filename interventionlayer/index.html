The Intervention Layer

We are building the layer products plug into.

Orcasho started as a real-world adaptive digestive system. As the prescription and feedback record accumulated, it revealed something bigger: a way to interpret what happens when a molecule, food, supplement or biological intervention meets a changing human.

01 · The bigger picture

Every industry owns an asset.
We are building the context around it.

Drug companies own molecules. AI drug discovery companies find molecules. Supplement companies own formulas. Microbiome companies own strains. Diagnostics companies own measurements. The missing piece is the changing human context connecting those assets to what actually happens next.

The shift

From product → to intervention layer. The goal is not to compete with every product touching the human body. The goal is to give those products a real-world layer in which their behaviour can be interpreted, tested and eventually predicted.

Static product model

Target → formulate → deliver → measure. The product is the endpoint. The human context is mostly outside the product's design loop.

Orcasho model

State → hypothesis → combination → 20-day intervention → response → next combination. Every cycle adds another labelled intervention-response observation.

02 · The intervention layer

One chemical language.
Four digestive gates.

The shareable model represents compounds in a common chemical framework and follows their changing behaviour across four pH-defined gates. The detailed encoding, thresholds and scoring remain confidential.

04 · How this was built

The layer was not the original destination.

It emerged from repeatedly running an adaptive prescription-feedback loop in the real world. The important asset is not the ingredient list. It is the accumulated sequence of changing combinations and observed responses.

The accidental discovery

We were building a treatment system.
The data started looking like an intervention layer.

Each cycle recorded what was given, to which patient state, what changed, and what was tried next. Because the combination changes every 20 days, the dataset is not just a collection of patient outcomes. It is a sequence of intervention-response observations. Over time, the same chemical language began to describe not only supplements, but also medications and food constituents.

That is the insight: the data we were generating to improve the treatment system can become the foundation for interpreting external interventions.

HOW THE DATA ENTERS THE SIMULATION

The percentages are observed inputs, not simulation outputs.

The accumulated prescription-response record is processed locally before candidate interventions are simulated. A Random Forest learns recurring relationships between prescription patterns and the response signals that follow them. Python then aggregates the observed prescription frequencies, recurring combinations and associated response weights into structured inputs for the mechanistic simulation.

01
Prescription historyRepeated real-world combinations and their observed frequencies.
02
Response learningRandom Forest identifies recurring prescription-response relationships.
03
Python aggregationFrequencies, recurring patterns and associated response weights become structured inputs.
04
SimulationThose empirical inputs inform candidate combinations before they are compared through the four gates.

In other words: the percentages come from the accumulated real prescription-response record. The simulation uses those structured empirical inputs together with the mechanistic model; it does not invent the percentages itself.

06 · How the data becomes the layer

It started with patients.
The next step is external interventions.

The original objective was practical: formulate and rotate combinations every 20 days so real patients could remain in their normal environment, including their existing medications and food, while symptoms and downstream markers were observed over time.

01
Real patientActual condition, medication, food and changing life context.
02
CombinationA multi-compound intervention is selected for that state.
03
20-day responseSymptoms and downstream measurements are recorded.
04
Next combinationThe prescription changes as the state changes.
05
Intervention recordThe sequence becomes the reference layer for the next intervention.

The simple idea

We kept trying to make the patient stable without making the patient's life artificial. The resulting data contains something unusual: intervention and response are recorded together, repeatedly, across changing real-world states.

What exists today

More than 300 patients, 1,510 feedback entries, repeated 20-day prescription cycles, a four-gate chemistry model and longitudinal patient trajectories. The detailed chemical encoding remains confidential.

What comes next

Introduce external molecules, strains, foods and products into this framework, generate explicit hypotheses, then test them prospectively against what actually happens in humans.

The layer in one line

Give Orcasho an intervention. The layer places it inside the changing human context, follows it through the digestive gates, connects it to real-world intervention-response data, and learns from what happens next.
