Evidence

Why the system, not one pill, is what we test

A fixed trial tests one locked combination. Orcasho changes the combination every cycle, so the system that does the changing is what needs proving.

How to read this section
Top diagram: the trial locks one combination while the patient keeps changing. The tiles show how far trials sit from real patients. The five-step flow is the validation plan.
What to take from it
A fixed trial answers a different question. Adaptive strategies are a recognised design, tested as a whole against standard care.

A fixed trial and an adaptive system answer different questions

The trial asks whether one locked combination works. Orcasho asks whether the method that keeps choosing the next combination works.

What each method is built to answer

Neither replaces the other. They answer different questions.

What the question needsConventional RCTOrcasho real-world record
A defined intervention against a comparator● strongest tool◐ next step, real-world comparator
Every real medicine, diet and condition together○ often excluded● present by design
A patient state that changes during treatment○ held fixed● followed every cycle
Acute events such as fever or infection○ usually excluded● recorded as they happen
Learning from one cycle to the next○ protocol is locked● the core mechanism

Adaptive treatment strategies are a recognised design

The strategy is developed, refined, then evaluated as a whole against standard care. The same idea runs through regulatory guidance.

Who already recognises the principle
Methods
SMART and dynamic treatment regimes: built to develop adaptive strategies, then test them against standard care.
FDA, 2019
Adaptive designs guidance for drugs and biologics: planned modifications are accepted when set in advance.
FDA, Dec 2024
Change-control plans for AI-enabled devices: the change rule and acceptance criteria are agreed up front.
ICH E20, 2025
Draft global guideline on adaptive designs, defined as prospectively planned modifications.
What this does not say. That randomised trials lack value.
What it does say. A fixed trial is not the whole picture for a patient who keeps changing. The system is what to test.
These frameworks cover trial designs and AI-enabled devices. What carries over is the principle: fix the change rule in advance, then test the system.

Where PK/PD and PBPK run out, and what this layer adds

Not instead of them. Alongside them. PK/PD and PBPK are the standard way to size a drug: a dose goes in and a blood level comes out. This layer reads everything a real patient takes and tells modelling which question to ask.

PK/PD and PBPKThis layer
1What it looks atOne drug at a time.Everything the patient takes, together, in one chemical language.
2Who the patient isAn average, virtual patient.The real patient: their medicines, diet and condition, left as they are.
3What goes inA dose and a time.Direction and dose: which way each item pushes the gut chemistry, gate by gate, and by how much.
4Over timeA snapshot at one state.Redone every 20 days, so it follows the patient as they change, for example while coming off a PPI.
5Where it learns fromTrials, where food and other medicines are controlled.Daily life: 300+ patients and 1,700+ prescriptions, each paired with the patient’s reply 20 days later.
6The question it answersHow much, for how long, at what blood level.Which way, at which gate, in this patient, and which pair needs sizing.
7What comes outA concentration curve.A gate-by-gate reading, refreshed every 20 days.
8Interactions between productsIncluded only if someone adds them. A zinc supplement binding an antibiotic is missed unless it is put in.Found across everything the patient takes, such as the zinc supplement against the antibiotic, and held for the whole course.
9Role of the patient’s replyUsed when a new study is run.Every reply shapes the next prescription, 20 days later.
10Role in the workflowSizes and validates one drug with confidence.Finds the pair and the moment that need sizing, hands them over, and learns from the result.
Together: this layer finds the question, PK/PD and PBPK size the answer, and each patient reply sharpens the next question. If you already run PK/PD or PBPK, this adds the inputs it does not have: what else is on the table and how the patient’s state has changed since the last run. The same pattern holds for every industry in section 9: you keep the asset you own, and the layer adds the context around it.
