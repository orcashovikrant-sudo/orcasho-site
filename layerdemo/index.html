What is tracked

PPI, antacid, laxative and metformin use and frequency, symptom scores, and blood markers where the patient shares them.

Who is counted

Every patient, including the ones who miss the target or land above or below it.

Why it holds in the real world

Patients pay for these outcomes. A missed target shows up in renewals, so the commercial record is a check on the clinical one.

Real-world observational data, not a controlled trial. A prospective pilot with outcomes agreed in advance is planned.

The chemistry provides the rules

pH decides how every compound behaves at each of the four gates. That is physical chemistry, so it holds for every patient and does not depend on data. It defines what a working gut looks like.

Patient data shows what matters

Within those rules, many combinations are possible. Every patient’s history shows which ones actually move the gut toward working. It feeds back into the four-gate model and is checked against it all the time, so each intervention sharpens it.

Local by design

Everything runs locally. Nothing sits anywhere else. The IP is owned by us, and even the language analysis model is custom made by us and runs locally.

Prescriptions are generated locally

Every draft prescription is made on our own systems, by our own models. No external API and no third-party model is involved.

Feedback is stored locally

Every piece of patient feedback, and the language read of it, is stored on our own systems alongside the patient’s record.
