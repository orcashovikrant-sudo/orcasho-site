Everything below models what happens once that loop is deliberately fed with capital: first with a US clinical pilot to validate it outside India, then with paid growth and institutional partnerships to scale it.

Capital efficiency is built into this number, not bolted on afterward. No hiring plan is baked into the $2M beyond a lean core of 3–4 hires, because the retention engine itself, the pattern-recognition and ML models that score and time every re-order, is developed and run entirely on our own infrastructure. Claude is used for operational tasks only (support workflows, internal reporting, content drafting), never for the IP that drives the model, so LLM/token spend stays under 10% of platform cost. That combination, a small team plus locally-owned algorithms, is why the same $800K/$700K/$500K split above buys a full clinical pilot, a paid growth engine, and an outreach motion, without a headcount-heavy burn rate eating into runway.
