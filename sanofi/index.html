03 · How it works

A system that recalibrates.

A 15-dimensional patient-state model evaluates candidate combinations from a 70–100 substance library through four pH-defined digestive gates. Each 20-day cycle connects the protocol to the next observed response.

01 · MAPSymptoms, history and clinical measures.
02 · MODELBuild the current digestive-state profile.
03 · SIMULATECompare candidate intervention combinations.
04 · OBSERVERecord symptoms and follow-up measures.
05 · ADAPTUse response to inform the next cycle.

04 · Why rural health

Continuity of care across rural distances.

Women’s health meets three rural barriers: distance, limited specialty access and fragmented follow-up. Orcasho’s model adds structured check-ins between visits, built with local clinics and community health partners.

Barriers we would prioritize

  • Specialty access: structured follow-up between appointments.
  • Distance: routine remote check-ins closer to home.
  • Continuity: longitudinal support through local care partners.

Local partnership

  • Work with a U.S. rural clinic, community health center or women’s-health provider.
  • Adapt the workflow with local patients and clinicians.
  • Agree on referral and escalation pathways.
  • Track engagement, follow-up completion and patient experience.
