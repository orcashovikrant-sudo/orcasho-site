ORCASHO INTELLIGENCE

Built on what actually happened

The same loop — corpus, feedback, retrieval, scale — that trains every large language model. Pointed at a much smaller, much more personal corpus. A directional, self-improving model — not a diagnosis. It gets more useful with every visit logged, and stays honest about what it doesn't know yet.
